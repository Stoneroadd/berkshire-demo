Discipline

02

Stewardship

Own businesses, not tickers. Treat every holding as a trust passed forward — to the next manager, the next generation, the next century.

03

Longevity

Compound patiently. The most powerful force in finance is not leverage or timing — it is the willingness to stay.

A CENTURY OF CONVICTION

Heritage is not history.
It is infrastructure.

1839

The Valley Falls Company is founded in Rhode Island — the earliest thread in the Berkshire tapestry.

1955

Berkshire Fine Spinning Associates merges with Hathaway Manufacturing — the combined entity struggles for years.

1965

Warren Buffett takes control of Berkshire Hathaway and begins transforming a failing textile mill into a holding empire.

1985

The last textile mill closes. The pivot from manufacturing to investing is complete — the old name carries a new mission.

2008

Amid global crisis, Berkshire invests $5 billion in Goldman Sachs preferred stock — conviction when others flee.

2024

Berkshire's market capitalization surpasses $1 trillion — a century of patience, discipline, and compounding made visible.
